    Reading and writing

    The library

    I read in French and English, from J. K. Rowling to Dan Brown. I also write: short stories, a fantasy novel and poems, including many Petrarchan sonnets in alexandrines.

    What I take from it: a respect for form and a taste for the right word, useful for writing clear documentation.

    Things to explore

    • 1 The pile of novels: J. K. Rowling and Dan Brown, in English as well as in French.
    • 2 The manuscript: a fantasy novel inspired by the worlds of Tolkien, J. K. Rowling, Narnia and Nanny McPhee.
    • 3 The poetry collection: Petrarchan sonnets in alexandrines and triolets, about nature, landscapes and memories.

    Films and video editing

    The screening room

    I love a laugh with Les Visiteurs or Louis de Funès’s Gendarme films, a thrill with The Da Vinci Code, and adventure with Indiana Jones or The Lord of the Rings. I also edit my own videos, mostly with Pinnacle Studio.

    What I take from it: telling a story in a short time, and a sense of pace for a demo or a project video.

    Things to explore

    • 1 The film reels: comedies (Opération Corned Beef, Le Pari), esoteric thrillers (Angels & Demons), fantasy (Harry Potter, The Hobbit, Charlie and the Chocolate Factory), animation (Elemental, Toy Story, Zootopia) and a little science fiction (Ready Player One).
    • 2 The editing desk: Pinnacle Studio above all; I have also tried Final Cut, Premiere Pro, Windows Movie Maker and PowToon.
    • 3 The clapperboard: videos for study projects and associations, short films and music clips.

    The studio

    Music, dance and drama

    The stage

    I sang for about ten years and learnt the drums, the violin and the piano. I have performed on stage in singing, dance and drama. These days it is karaoke and 80s-90s hits, which I cheered on from the front row at Stars 80 vs Stars 90 : La Grande Battle.

    What I take from it: confidence when speaking in front of an audience, and listening to the group.

    Things to explore

    • 1 The music stand: about ten years of singing: a choir at primary school, then an association and a municipal music school.
    • 2 The instruments: drums (2 years, municipal music school), violin (2 years, private lessons), piano (1 year, self-taught then at a private music school).
    • 3 The dance shoes: a folk dance club and a Zumba club.
    • 4 The concert ticket: Stars 80 vs Stars 90 : La Grande Battle, 7 June 2025, front row, broadcast live on the French TV channel M6.
    • 5 The microphone: for karaoke nights.

    Horse riding

    The tack room

    In the saddle from 1999 to 2022, with three horses of my own. In inter-school competitions (secondary level), I ranked first in the region (Nord-Pas-de-Calais).

    What I take from it: regular habits, calm under pressure and a sense of responsibility.

    Things to explore

    • 1 The three stable name plates: one for each of my horses.
    • 2 The rosettes: pony games (gymkhana and show jumping), show jumping and carousel (a musical ride with basic dressage).
    • 3 The riding hat and boots: put away in 2022, after 20 km endurance rides with my last mare.
  8. Image coming soon

    Nature, adventure and mapping

    The adventurer’s refuge

    Before computing, I was a nature guide and a tour guide, and I led a team of ten on a mapping assignment. On walks, I still try to identify every plant and understand what it does for living things. Backpack on, I have travelled through 88 of the 96 départements of mainland France and Corsica, and 22 European countries and territories.

    What I take from it: independence, a good sense of direction, and the knack of explaining complex things simply.

    Things to explore

    • 1 The annotated map: the mapping assignment, with a team of ten.
    • 2 The binoculars and magnifying glass: a two-year multilingual nature guide diploma (DEUST, a French university diploma), and training as a game and fishing warden (swearing-in to come).
    • 3 The backpack: from my guided tours for the 4 Rivières and Sambre-Avesnois tourist offices.
    • 4 The camera and the telescope: landscapes photographed to promote places (I have created tourist documentation and signage), and astronomy on clear nights.
    • 5 The map of my travels: see the map · Andorra, Austria, Belgium, the Czech Republic, Denmark, England, Finland, Germany, Hungary, Ireland, Latvia, Luxembourg, Monaco, the Netherlands, Portugal, Slovakia, Spain, Switzerland and the Vatican, not forgetting Jersey and Gibraltar.

    Handywoman and inventor: I love repairing, making and coming up with my own solutions, from hand tools to the keyboard.

    Volunteering and community

    The common room

    Volunteer work camps in France, Germany and England meant so much to me that I went on to lead one: they changed my life. Since then, I get involved wherever I am: tutoring and language cafés at the University of Lille, then the student union, the IUT council and the eco-delegates in Lens.

    What I take from it: working with very different people, and moving a group forward.

    Things to explore

    • 1 The work-camp suitcase: volunteer camps in France, Germany and England, one of which I led.
    • 2 The language-café mugs: University of Lille, Cité Scientifique campus: six years of language cafés, including one year running them as a tandem tutor, plus English tutoring for the disability support service.
    • 3 The greeter badge: Lille in English, German, Spanish and Dutch.
    • 4 The notice board: student union (BDE), elected member of the IUT council (and of the department council in my first year), coordinator of student eco-delegates for the University of Artois sustainability and CSR service.
    • 5 The twinning pennant: Les Amis de Buxton (France–UK) since 2024, and formerly Latinos en Lille.
