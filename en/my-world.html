    horse riding

    The tack room

    In the saddle from 1999 to 2022: over twenty years of riding and three horses of my own. In inter-school competitions (secondary level), I ranked first in the region (Nord-Pas-de-Calais). My disciplines: pony games (gymkhana and show jumping), show jumping, carousel (a musical ride with basic dressage) and, with my last mare, endurance riding over 20 km.

    Things to explore

    • 1 The three stable name plates: one for each of my horses.
    • 2 The rosettes: first place in the regional inter-school competitions.
    • 3 The riding hat and boots: put away since 2022.
  5. image coming soon

    nature, adventure and mapping

    The adventurer’s refuge

    Before computing, I was a nature guide and a tour guide (4 Rivières and Sambre-Avesnois tourist offices), after graduating top of my class in a two-year multilingual nature guide diploma (DEUST, a French university diploma). I led a team of ten on a mapping assignment. Backpack on, map in hand: I am an adventurer at heart, with 88 of the 96 départements of mainland France and Corsica and 20 European countries under my belt, from Portugal to Finland.

    Things to explore

    • 1 The annotated map: a keepsake from the mapping assignment.
    • 2 The binoculars and magnifying glass: a naturalist’s kit.
    • 3 The backpack: always ready to go.
    • 4 The map of my travels: Andorra, Austria, Belgium, the Czech Republic, Denmark, England, Finland, Germany, Hungary, Ireland, Latvia, Luxembourg, Monaco, the Netherlands, Portugal, Slovakia, Spain, Switzerland and the Vatican, not forgetting Jersey and Gibraltar.
