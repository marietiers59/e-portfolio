Accessibility

This portfolio aims to meet the RGAA 4.1.2 (the French accessibility standard), which follows WCAG 2.1 level AA. Here is where it stands, and how I checked it.

Results as of 8 November 2026

  • 98.5%of applicable RGAA criteria met (66 out of 67)
  • 0 errorsin automated WCAG 2.2 A and AA tests (axe-core)
  • 100 / 100Lighthouse accessibility score, on every page

The site partially conforms to RGAA 4.1.2: only one criterion is not yet met (see below).

WCAG and RGAA have no official “score”: the percentage above is the conformance rate defined by RGAA, the share of criteria met among those that apply to the site. The Lighthouse score only measures what a robot can test.

Method

  • Self-assessment carried out by me, not an audit by an independent body.
  • Pages assessed: home, projects, skills, video CV, accessibility and personal area, plus their French versions.
  • Automated tests: axe-core 4.10 (WCAG 2.2 A and AA rules) on desktop and mobile, in light and dark themes; Lighthouse 12; W3C HTML validator.
  • Manual tests: keyboard-only navigation, 200% zoom, 320 px wide display, increased text spacing, contrast of text over images measured pixel by pixel, all 106 criteria reviewed one by one.
  • Environment: Chromium (the engine behind Chrome and Edge) on Linux.

What is not accessible yet

  • English CV (PDF): the file is untagged, so a screen reader may read it out of order (criterion 13.3). The French CV is tagged, and the main content of the CV is also on the pages of this site.

Points to watch

  • The contact form may be protected by Cloudflare Turnstile: the check needs no action from visitors and relies on no image to decipher.
  • The video CV is under construction: it will have subtitles and a written transcript.
